# Data policy and sources

The repository includes processed historical/interim tables, a paired-return beta dataset, the dated Damodaran methodology workbook, a ChinaBond observation payload, and the H6 source-download manifest. These files are sufficient for the released model verifiers.

Midea annual and interim reports, the convertible-bond notice, the S&P rating report, and raw market/provider payloads remain in the private reproduction archive where redistribution may be restricted or would make the clone unnecessarily large. `FINAL_SOURCE_REGISTRY_R3.csv` records every source's publisher, official URL, dates, original archived SHA-256, transformation, allowed use, and distribution status. To reconstruct an excluded source, download it from the recorded URL and require its SHA-256 to match before use.

Operating information cutoff: **2026-08-20**. Valuation market-data date: **2026-09-11**. Source attribution belongs to Midea Group, ChinaBond/CCDC, Aswath Damodaran and NYU Stern, Yahoo Finance and the identified market-data providers. No ownership of third-party source material is claimed.
