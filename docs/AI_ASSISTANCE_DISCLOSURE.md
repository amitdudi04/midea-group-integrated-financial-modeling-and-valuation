# AI assistance disclosure

This is an author-led finance research project. AI-assisted tools were used as development aids for coding support, debugging, documentation editing, repository organization, and repeatable validation.

Research design, source selection, accounting judgments, forecasting assumptions, valuation methodology, interpretation, and final conclusions remain the author's responsibility.

Material financial outputs are reproducible from source-bound inputs and independently recalculated through executable controls; no result is accepted solely because it was produced or suggested by an AI system.
