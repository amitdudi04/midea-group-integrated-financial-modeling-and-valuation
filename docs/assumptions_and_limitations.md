# Assumptions and limitations

- Company operating information is limited to facts available by 20 August 2026. Market inputs use 11 September 2026.
- Forecasts are scenario estimates, not company guidance or investment advice.
- Provider-reported peer financials remain source-native and are not fully restated across GAAP frameworks.
- Selected peer periods follow a governed TTM/latest-quarter basis; P/B is point-in-time. EBITDA was excluded from Midea implied comps where a compatible Q1 depreciation and amortization denominator was unavailable.
- The base DCF is sensitive to WACC and terminal growth; terminal value is approximately 88.8% of enterprise value.
- The wide difference between DCF and comps supports a low-to-medium confidence rating and a high risk rating.
- Third-party reports and market payloads remain owned by their publishers. Some are retained only in the private reproduction archive and are identified publicly by URL and SHA-256.
