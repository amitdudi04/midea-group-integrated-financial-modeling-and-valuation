# Model architecture

```text
Primary Sources
      ↓
Historical Financials
      ↓
Interim 2026Q1 Bridge
      ↓
Forecast Drivers
      ↓
Integrated Three-Statement Model
      ↓
Operating NWC / FCFF
      ↓
WACC
      ↓
DCF
      ↓
Comparable Companies
      ↓
Valuation Synthesis
      ↓
Excel / Dashboard / Reports
      ↓
Independent Verification
```

The internal authority labels H1 through H7 identify, respectively, historical statements, the interim bridge, forecast mechanics, WACC, DCF, comparable companies, and synthesis. They are retained in filenames for traceability; the flow above is the intended academic reading order.
