# Methodology

Historical statements are normalized from Midea's published filings without replacing source-native precision with invented exactness. The 2026 Q1 bridge separates actual facts from forecast assumptions. Revenue, margins, working-capital drivers, capital expenditure, financing, tax, and distributions flow through an integrated three-statement model.

FCFF equals `EBIT × (1 − effective tax rate) + depreciation + amortization − capex − Δ operating NWC − Δ financial-services net investment`. Operating NWC uses eight operating asset accounts and seven operating liability accounts. Financial-services net investment equals current loans and advances plus the finance portion of current non-current assets, less customer deposits.

WACC uses market-value weights, a dated RMB risk-free rate, beta estimated from paired monthly Midea and Shanghai Composite returns, the 1 July 2026 Damodaran China ERP, and a ChinaBond marginal borrowing proxy. DCF discounts 2026E–2030E FCFF and a Gordon-growth terminal value. Comparable-company multiples use selected eligible peers on a peer-TTM/latest-quarter to Midea-LTM basis; P/B is point-in-time. Valuation synthesis treats DCF as primary and comps as a reasonableness check without mechanically averaging them.
